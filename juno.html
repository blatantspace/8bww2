The shelf · Empty stage · Battlegrounds · Juno Beach · 6 June 1944

Juno Beach

21,400 men came ashore here. Each figure is one of them. The sprites are small so the whole landing fits. Sea is at the top. They walk inland, toward the villages.

Drawing the landing.

About 14,000 were Canadian. The rest were British. A thin German line waits in the grass. Same figures as the shelf, just smaller. Blatant Space.
